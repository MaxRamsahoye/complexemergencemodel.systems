The Complex Emergence Model

A successor to Schmachtenberger’s Emergence Model that doubles the civilisational structures to eight – four at the core, two interfaces with ‘second nature’ and two that transect them all – for understanding the conditioning of human agency and the generative dynamics of the metacrisis.

Civilisational Structures

The relatively enduring patterns of social relations, institutions, technologies and meanings – and the bodies and ecosystems beneath them – that constrain and enable human action, generating both human agency and existential risk.
