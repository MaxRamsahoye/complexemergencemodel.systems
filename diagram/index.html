Models

A genealogy of the frameworks that model civilisation as interacting structures. Marx’s base and superstructure set the pattern; Harris’s cultural materialism added a third layer, Wilber’s four quadrants a second axis, and Schmachtenberger’s Emergence Model brought the two together as the structures that condition human behaviour. The Complex Emergence Model and its Exo-Endo Structure carry the line forward: eleven structures, each shaping human agency from without and internalised within it.

The claim that competition is the mechanism for technological determinism is a special case of the theory of selectionism (also called Universal Darwinism). Selectionism generalizes the mechanism at the heart of evolutionary biology: variation and differential proliferation. In a population of varied forms, if some forms proliferate (reproduce, survive, grow) because of some characteristic, then that characteristic will be more abundant in future populations. Over time, these forms, called organisms in evolutionary biology and interactors more generally, will fit better with—will appear as if designed for—the criterion that determines superior proliferation (the “selection environment”).

Allan Dafoe, On Technological Determinism (2015)
